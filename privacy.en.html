Privacy information

Controller and contact

Alguer Balanyà is the controller of the personal data provided by people contacting Arenatec. For privacy enquiries or to exercise your rights, email arenatec.info@gmail.com.

Enquiries

We use the information you provide by email or WhatsApp to handle and respond to your enquiry. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in handling enquiries received. We retain enquiries for one year after the last communication and then delete them.

Email is handled through Gmail. If you choose WhatsApp, communications take place through that service. Please consult the providers' information about their processing and possible international transfers: Google and WhatsApp.

Visiting the website

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. According to GitHub, the service logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Pages information and its privacy statement. Links to Instagram and WhatsApp lead to external services with their own policies.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of your data, object to processing and request portability where applicable. Contact us at the email address above. You may also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency.
